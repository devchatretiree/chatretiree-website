ChatRetiree privacy policy

Last updated November 28, 2026

The short version

  • We look at what you share, show you what we found, and then let it go. We don’t keep your messages, pictures, links or questions, unless you choose to send them to us in a note.
  • We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and never share it so anyone can market to you.

What we keep

  • Your email address, so you can sign in with a code. If we invited you, your email address is also kept with your invitation.
  • A short record of each check, question or message on Home: the date, the type (for example a message or a link), which result or next step we picked, which of our services helped, and how long it took. It is tied to your account so we can apply daily limits. It never includes your message, picture, link, question or our answer. We delete these records after 90 days.
  • Your answers to “How was this check?”, if you give them. They are deleted along with the record of that check.
  • When you check a message, we may add one to a daily count of the kind of scam it looks like (for example, an unpaid toll text). We store only the kind of scam and the date. We don’t store the message, links, names or numbers, and the count is not linked to you or your account. Counts are deleted after 90 days.
  • When you open some parts of the app (like Scams going around or Get help after a scam), tap a next step, or use a text size or light or dark setting, we add one to a daily count for that choice. We store only the date, the choice (from a fixed list) and the number, never who it was. Your settings are counted at most once a day. Counts are deleted after 90 days.
  • Our team sees only totals made from these records, like how many people used a scam check this week or how many new members joined. We never look at one person’s records.

What stays on your phone

  • Your text size and your light or dark choice. We only count which ones are in use, as described above.
  • The people you trust, if you add them. Their names and email addresses are never sent to us.
  • The year you were born, if you tell us. We ask only when the answer to a Medicare or Social Security question depends on your age, and never for your full birthday. It stays on your phone and is never sent to us. You can see it or forget it in Settings.
  • Your sign-in, so you don’t need a new code every time, and the email address you last used.
  • Pictures you choose. The words in a picture are read on your phone (or in your web browser), and only the words you confirm are checked.
  • We don’t use your location. “Find my local police” opens your own Maps app, and we never see where you are.

What happens when you check something

  • Before anything leaves your phone, we hide Social Security numbers, Medicare numbers, card numbers, passwords and security codes. We also try to hide your bank account number, date of birth, home address, and your own phone number or email address, and you can put those back if we guessed wrong. Names and other details are not hidden, so feel free to remove anything you’d rather not share.
  • The words are sent over an encrypted connection to our checking service, checked for warning signs, and then let go. We don’t store them.
  • A link is looked up with Google’s Web Risk service, which tells us if Google knows it to be harmful. We never open the link. If a link seems to hold a private code, we offer to check only the website name.
  • The words also get a second look from an AI service. Today that is Google’s Gemini, used under paid terms that don’t let Google use your words to train its AI. Google may keep what it receives for a short time to prevent misuse. The AI can’t open links or contact anyone.

Medicare, Social Security and scam questions

  • We hide private details in your question the same way. Then we find the matching parts of official Medicare or Social Security guides, or of official alerts from the FTC, the FBI and the Office of Inspector General at the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. The AI service explains them in plain words.
  • If your question depends on your age and you told us the year you were born, your phone works out an age range (for example “62 to 64”) and only that range goes along with that question, to help find the right parts of the guides. The year itself never leaves your phone, and the range is not stored.
  • Your question and our answer are not stored. We keep only the short record described above.

Talking with ChatRetiree on Home

  • When you write to ChatRetiree on Home, we hide private details first, the same way. Then the last few messages of your conversation go to the same AI service, which works out what you need and writes a short reply, like a question back to you. It doesn’t answer from its own knowledge: answers still come from the scam check, official guides, official alerts and official help pages.
  • The conversation stays only in the app’s memory on your phone. It’s cleared when you close the app, sign out, or tap the ChatRetiree logo on Home. We don’t store it.

When you tell us how it went

  • If you send us a note with “Tell us” or Contact us, we send it to our team by email. It holds only what you choose: the kind of note you picked, any words you add (with private details hidden first, the same way), which part of the app you were in, your language and the app version.
  • The end of your conversation goes along only if you turn on “Include our conversation”, and you see it before you send. Your email address goes along only if you say we can email you back.
  • Your note isn’t stored in the app or in our database. It stays in our team’s email inbox, and we use it only to answer you and to improve ChatRetiree.
  • So nobody can send too many, we keep the time you sent a note, tied to your account, for 7 days. It never includes what you wrote.

Speaking and listening

  • If you tap Speak, your phone’s own speech service (Apple’s or Google’s), or your web browser’s, turns your voice into words. Some phones do this on the phone itself. Others send the sound to Apple or Google to do it, under their own privacy terms. We never receive the recording, only the words you choose to check.
  • Read aloud uses your phone’s own voice. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Who can see your checks

  • Nobody else. Not family members, not anyone who pays for your account, and not our team, because we don’t keep what you checked.
  • If you tap “Ask someone I trust”, your own email app opens with the result and the message. Nothing is sent until you press send there, and it doesn’t go through us.
  • If you copy a summary, it goes only to your phone’s clipboard. Links and numbers are taken out of it.

Companies that help us

  • Supabase runs our accounts and checking service and holds the records described above.
  • Resend sends the email with your sign-in code, and the notes you send with “Tell us”.
  • Google Workspace runs our team’s email, where those notes arrive.
  • Google looks up links (Web Risk) and gives the AI second look (Gemini).
  • Expo delivers the app and its updates, and hosts the web version.
  • Like most online services, these companies keep short technical logs, such as your internet (IP) address and the time of a request, to keep things running and secure. We don’t use these logs to track you.

Deleting your account

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This removes your email address, invitation and feedback, and clears the people you trust and the year you were born from this phone.
  • Records of past checks lose any link to you and are deleted within 90 days. We keep them that long only so the service stays within its daily budget.

Children

  • ChatRetiree is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we’ll delete the account.

Questions and changes

  • Email support@chatretiree.com with any question about your privacy. If we change this policy, we’ll show the new version in the app and update the date at the top.
