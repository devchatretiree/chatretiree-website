Why we think so

If you’re worried someone you love is in trouble

  1. If someone may be in danger right now, call 911.
  2. Hang up or stop replying. Then call your family member yourself, using a number you already have. If you can’t reach them, call another relative or a friend who would know.
  3. Don’t send any money, gift cards or cryptocurrency, not even part of what they ask for.
  4. To report it, contact your local police.

If someone may be in danger, call 911.

What we’d do next

  1. Don’t send money. Hang up and call your family member, or another relative, on a number you already have.

Why we think so

  • It asks for payment with gift cards. Only scammers ask to be paid this way.
  • It tells you to keep it secret. Scammers do this so no one can warn you.
  • It describes a family emergency that needs money fast. This is a common way to cause panic.
