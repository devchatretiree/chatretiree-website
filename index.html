How it works

No forms and no jargon. Just a few taps.

  1. Copy it

    Copy the text or email that worries you, or take a picture of your screen.

  2. Paste it in

    Open ChatRetiree and tap “Paste and check.” That’s it.

  3. Talk it over

    We’ll show you the warning signs we found, in plain words, and what you can do next.

Your privacy comes first

  • We don’t keep your messages, pictures, links or questions.
  • Before anything leaves your phone, we hide things like Social Security, Medicare and card numbers.
  • We never sell your information or use it for ads.
