What ChatRetiree Does

One friendly place for it all

Just ask what’s on your mind

Start with one box. Type a question, paste a message that seems off, or just say it out loud. We’ll take it to the right place, whether that’s a scam check, Medicare, Social Security, taxes, veterans benefits or help after a scam.

  • One message bar everywhere: tap + to paste a message or add a picture, or tap the mic to say it
  • Not sure where to start? Tap Scam check, Medicare or Social Security
  • In English or Spanish
  • Every answer ends with where to go next, so it all fits together

Check a message before you act

Paste a text or email, add a picture of your screen, or just tell us what happened. In a few seconds you’ll see what we found and exactly what to do next. It’s more than an opinion: every message goes through real checks.

  • Looks for the warning signs scammers rely on
  • Checks links against Google’s list of known dangerous websites, without opening them
  • Compares it with the latest scam alerts from the FTC, FBI and HHS
  • Never calls anything “safe.” When we can’t tell, we say so

Straight answers about your benefits

Ask about Medicare or Social Security in your own words, like you would ask a friend who knows them inside out. You can also ask about retirement taxes, veterans benefits, help programs like SNAP, heating help and Lifeline, and staying in your home. Answers come only from official government guides, and you can see the exact words they’re based on.

  • Plain-language answers in a simple back-and-forth, like texting a friend. Type it or just say it
  • Every point checked against the official guide
  • If the answer depends on your age, we ask the year you were born. It stays on your phone
  • For your own plan or benefits, we point you to the free official help, like 1‑800‑MEDICARE, your local SHIP counselor, Social Security or the VA
See sample Medicare questions

Know what’s going around

We follow the official scam warnings from the FTC, the FBI and the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, and sum each one up in plain words. Ask about a scam and get an answer straight from those warnings.

  • New warnings summarized as they come out
  • When the warnings don’t cover something, we say so instead of guessing
  • We never suggest paying a scammer anything

Help after a scam

It happens to careful, smart people every day, and it’s not your fault. Just tap Scam check and say what happened, like sending money or sharing your Social Security number, and we’ll work out the next steps together, one at a time.

  • Steps from official sources like the FTC and IdentityTheft.gov, with links to their own websites
  • Reach Social Security, Medicare, the IRS or the Postal Service yourself, using their official contacts
  • If you paid, the steps fit how you paid, like a gift card, bank transfer or app
  • Also steps to freeze your credit, cut down on robocalls, and what to do after losing a loved one

Decide together

Add a family member or friend you trust. After any check, one tap opens your own email with a note to them, so you can talk it over before doing anything.

  • You see the note first, and nothing is sent until you press send
  • Links are broken on purpose so nobody taps them by accident
  • Your trusted people stay on your phone, never on our servers

Think you’ve been scammed?

You’re not alone, and it’s not your fault.

  • If someone may be in danger, call 911.
  • If you already sent money or gift cards, call your bank or the gift card company right away.
  • You can also report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
  • In the app, just tap Scam check and say what happened. It walks you through the next steps, one at a time.
