Why we built ChatRetiree

You’ve spent a lifetime figuring things out. The trouble isn’t you.

It’s the flood of fake texts, look-alike emails, robocalls and fine print that lands on everyone, every day. ChatRetiree cuts through it, so you spend less time wondering and worrying.

There’s too much noise

Scam texts and fake alerts are everywhere, and they look more like the real thing every year.

Good answers are buried

The right information is out there, spread across long guides and official websites that take time to dig through.

Wondering takes time

One “Is this real?” moment can eat up an afternoon. You have better things to do with your day.

What we believe

Everyone deserves easy access to clear, trustworthy information, and a quick way to check before they act. So ChatRetiree answers only from official sources, in plain words, and shows you where each answer comes from.

What ChatRetiree does

Four ways we make life simpler

Check a message before you act

Paste a text or email, add a picture of your screen, or just tell us what happened. In a few seconds you’ll see what we noticed and exactly what to do next.

  • Looks for the warning signs scammers rely on
  • Checks links against Google’s list of harmful sites, without opening them
  • Never calls anything “safe.” When we can’t tell, we say so
See four real samples

Straight answers about Medicare

Ask in your own words, like you would ask a friend who knows Medicare inside out. Answers come only from the official Medicare guides, and you can see the guide’s exact words.

  • Plain-language answers, with follow-up questions
  • Every point checked against the official guide
  • For your own plan, we point you to 1‑800‑MEDICARE or your free local SHIP counselor
See sample questions

Know what’s going around

We follow the official scam warnings from the FTC, the FBI and the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, and sum each one up in plain words. Ask about a scam and get an answer straight from those warnings.

  • New warnings summarized as they come out
  • When the warnings don’t cover something, we say so instead of guessing
  • We never suggest paying a scammer anything
See sample questions

Decide together

Add a family member or friend you trust. After any check, one tap opens your own email with a note to them, so you can talk it over before doing anything.

  • You see the note first, and nothing is sent until you press send
  • Links are broken on purpose so nobody taps them by accident
  • Your trusted people stay on your phone, never on our servers
